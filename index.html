dMAT Practice

An unofficial, open-source mock exam

Practice the format of the digital Master Test (dMAT) — the study aptitude test used by German universities to admit international applicants to Master's programmes. Timed, single-choice exercises modeled on the official Core Module and Subject Module structure.

Not affiliated with g.a.s.t. or dMAT. This is an independent, open-source practice tool built from the publicly available dMAT preparatory materials (d-mat.de). All puzzles here are freshly generated, original content — nothing is copied from the official materials. No accounts, no tracking, no server: everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved beyond your current tab.
